## Part 4: NBER Working Paper Track

Getting an NBER working paper number builds credibility before journal acceptance.

**Path 1: Direct affiliation**
- Requires being an NBER Research Associate or Faculty Research Fellow
- Usually requires tenure-track position at research university

**Path 2: Co-author route**
- Co-author with an NBER affiliate who can post it
- Most realistic path for you

**Path 3: Present at NBER conference**
- Papers presented at NBER conferences can be posted as working papers
- Your paper fits the **Productivity, Innovation, and Entrepreneurship** program
